# 0002: Show the timer, never cut people off

**Status:** accepted, 0.1.0

## Context

Hard cut-offs make standups robotic and people resent them. No timer at all lets them drift.

## Decision

The timer turns red and the line says "over, park it?" when time is up, with an optional short sound. The facilitator decides when to move on.

## Tradeoff

- Keeps the social norm with the team, not the tool.
- Overruns still happen, so they are counted in History as a share of turns over time.
- Teams that want stricter rules can lower the minutes per person.
